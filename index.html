Carom — 3D Pool

A 3D 8-ball pool game. Pocket all of your group (solids or stripes), then sink the 8-ball to win.

  • Aim: move the mouse over the table — the cue follows.
  • Shoot: hold left mouse button to charge power, release to strike.
  • Spin (english): click inside the spin circle to set tip offset.
  • Rotate camera: drag with the right mouse button.
  • Zoom: mouse wheel.
  • Ball in hand: after a foul, click an open spot to place the cue ball.

Scratch (potting the cue ball), hitting the wrong group first, or driving no ball to a rail is a foul — your opponent gets ball in hand. Pocket the 8 too early, or scratch on the 8, and you lose.

Use vs Computer / 2 Player to choose your opponent, and AI: Easy / Medium / Hard to set the computer's skill.

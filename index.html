Carom — 3D Pool

A 3D 8-ball pool game. Pocket all of your group (solids or stripes), then sink the 8-ball to win.

  • Aim: click the table once to take the cue. The camera sits behind it, and moving the mouse left or right swings your aim round the cue ball. Hold Shift for fine aiming.
  • View: move the mouse forward or back to lower or raise the camera; the mouse wheel zooms.
  • Shoot: click to lock your aim, then pull the mouse back to set power. Release to strike if you're holding the button, or click again if you let go. Click without pulling, or right-click, to cancel.
  • Spin (english): arrow keys, or click inside the spin circle.
  • Free the cursor: press Esc to reach the buttons; click the table to take the cue again.
  • Ball in hand: after a foul, click an open spot to place the cue ball.

Scratch (potting the cue ball), hitting the wrong group first, or driving no ball to a rail is a foul — your opponent gets ball in hand. Pocket the 8 too early, or scratch on the 8, and you lose.

Use vs Computer / 2 Player to choose your opponent, and AI: Easy / Medium / Hard to set the computer's skill.
